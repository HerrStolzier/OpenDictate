Guides

Blink shows you how.

Five short videos, none longer than 17 seconds. Blink speaks German; the subtitles and the text under each video are in English.

How to dictate

What Blink says

“Hey, I'm Blink. Let me show you how to dictate. Hold down your key and just start talking. Let go, and I'll write it down. Done. Your text is right where you were.”

Dictate for free

What Blink says

“Dictating doesn't have to cost a cent. Enter your own server in the settings. Then your recording stays on your computer. And with OpenAI, a dictation costs about a tenth of a cent.”

How to set up a local model (in German)

Translated as you go

What Blink says

“You speak German, I write English. Pick your target language and just speak German. I'll translate it for you. And there it is in English. Perfect for gaming chat.”

Nothing gets lost

What Blink says

“Network down? No drama. Oops. Your recording is still saved. As soon as it works again, click Nochmal senden (Send again). And your text is there.”

First steps on the Mac

What Blink says

“You'll be ready in a minute. Download the app and drag it to Applications. Enter your key, or your own server. Pick a shortcut, and off you go.”
