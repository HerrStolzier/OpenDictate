How it works

Watch Blink do it.

  1. 01How to dictate

    Hold a key, talk, let go. Your text appears where your cursor is: in an email, a chat or a document.

  2. 02Translated as you go

    You speak English; out comes German or one of eleven other languages. For emails abroad or gaming chat. The Mac's built-in dictation doesn't translate.

  3. 03Nothing gets lost

    If the network drops or the service hiccups, your recording stays on your computer. Click “Nochmal senden” (Send again) to send it, any time within 24 hours.

  4. 04Free with your own server

    Enter a server running on your computer. Then a dictation costs nothing, and the recording stays with you.

  5. 05First steps on the Mac

    Download the app, drag it to Applications, enter a key or server, pick a shortcut. That's it.
